Health Benefits of Coffee

  • Protects the brain: Regular intake is associated with a lower risk of cognitive decline, Alzheimer's disease, and Parkinson's disease.
  • Supports liver health: Coffee helps guard against chronic liver disease, fatty liver, and liver cancer.
  • Lowers diabetes risk: Plant compounds and antioxidants in coffee help improve blood sugar balance and reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes.
  • Promotes heart health: Moderate consumption is linked to a lower risk of stroke and heart disease.
  • Provides antioxidants: Coffee is rich in polyphenols that reduce internal inflammation and fight cell damage.
